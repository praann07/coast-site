Features

Why Coast?

Built for people who share routes. Whether you commute daily or travel occasionally, Coast makes ride sharing effortless.

Nearby Trips

See ride posts on a live map. Find drivers heading your way, sorted by distance and departure time.

Instant Requests

Tap a trip, send a request. The driver gets notified instantly. Accept, decline, or negotiate in real time.

Split the Cost

Share fuel costs with co-passengers. Everyone saves money, and the driver offsets travel expenses.

How It Works

Three steps to ride

Coast is simple by design. Post, match, ride.

1

Post your trip

Enter your destination, time, and available seats. Your trip appears on the live map for nearby riders.

2

Get matched

Riders send requests. You accept the ones that work. Everyone gets real-time notifications.

3

Ride together

Meet at the pickup point, ride together, split the cost. Rate your co-passenger to build trust.
