Delete Your Coast Account

Last updated: August 2026

You can delete your Coast account and its data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Coast and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email support@coastnow.in from the address on your Coast account, with the subject Account deletion request.

We will verify the request comes from the account's registered email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

DataWhat it is
Account & profileYour name, email address, phone number and join date
TripsEvery trip you posted, including its approximate location
Ride requestsRequests you sent and requests you received
Ride historyYour record of shared rides
ReportsReports you filed about others, and reports others filed about you
Notification tokenThe device token used to send you push notifications

Nothing is retained. Every record listed above is removed from our database when your account is deleted. We keep no anonymised copy, no shadow profile, and no backup of your personal data beyond routine database backups, which are overwritten on a rolling 7-day cycle.

What this does not cover

Coast does not process payments and never holds your financial data, so there is nothing of that kind to delete.

If you exchanged phone numbers with another rider through Coast, that number now sits in their phone and their call history. Deleting your Coast account cannot reach it. Contact that person directly if you want them to remove it.

Questions about deletion or your data: support@coastnow.in
