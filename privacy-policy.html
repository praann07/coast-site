Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Coast ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Coast ride-matching mobile application (the "App"). This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: your full name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Trip information: destination, departure time, number of seats, and your origin location when you post a trip.
  • Ride requests: when you request or accept a ride, we record the connection between riders and drivers.
  • Communications: if you report another user, we record the report and the associated trip.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Precise GPS location: we collect your device's precise location in the foreground to show nearby trips, calculate distances, and keep your trip position updated. We do not collect location while the App is in the background.
  • Device information: we collect your push notification token for sending ride alerts.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: destination place search queries are sent to Nominatim, OpenStreetMap's geocoding service, to provide autocomplete suggestions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To match riders with drivers based on location and trip details.
  • To send push notifications about ride requests, acceptances, and declines.
  • To show nearby trips on a map.
  • To improve the App and fix bugs.
  • To ensure safety — ride request records and user reports help us moderate the community.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area, we process your data based on:

  • Consent: we ask for your permission before collecting precise GPS location.
  • Contractual necessity: account creation and ride matching are necessary to provide our service.
  • Legitimate interests: service improvement and abuse prevention.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following third-party service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database hosting, authentication, and realtime features.
  • Expo — push notification delivery and build infrastructure.
  • Nominatim — place search autocomplete for Indian destinations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as you maintain an active account. Trip and ride request records are retained for service continuity and safety moderation. Deleting your account removes all of it immediately — see Delete Your Account.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking at any time via your device settings.
  • Opt out of push notifications via your device settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@coastnow.in.

You can delete your account and all associated data yourself, immediately, from the Profile tab in the App. See Delete Your Account for exactly what is removed.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication via Supabase (email OTP), and limited access controls to protect your data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated within the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:

support@coastnow.in
